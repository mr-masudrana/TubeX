TubeX is a YouTube client for Android built with Jetpack Compose, Media3 and NewPipe Extractor. No Google account, no ads, no analytics.

<b>Playback</b>
• Quality switching, background playback, Picture-in-Picture, Cast
• Playback speed, chapters, subtitles with size/color/background options
• SponsorBlock, DeArrow, Return YouTube Dislike
• Gestures for seek, volume and brightness, sleep timer, audio-only mode

<b>On-device recommendations</b>
• Learns from what you watch, skip and like, entirely on your phone
• Weekday/weekend and time-of-day patterns, topic variety
• Dashboard: see what it knows and why a video was suggested; export, reduce or wipe it

<b>Library</b>
• History, favorites, playlists, subscriptions, continue watching, downloads
• Import from NewPipe, back up your profile

11 themes, Bengali and English interface. Free software under GPL-3.0.
